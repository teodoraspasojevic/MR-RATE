# Acknowledging Resource Usage -- NHR@FAU HPC Documentation

Source: https://doc.nhr.fau.de/acknowledgment/

When publishing research that utilized NHR@FAU resources, researchers must include appropriate acknowledgments based on which resource category they accessed.

## Tier3 Grundversorgung Resources

> "The authors gratefully acknowledge the scientific support and HPC resources provided by the Erlangen National High Performance Computing Center (NHR@FAU) of the Friedrich-Alexander-Universitat Erlangen-Nurnberg (FAU). The hardware is partially funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG)."

## NHR@FAU Project Resources

> "The authors gratefully acknowledge the scientific support and HPC resources provided by the Erlangen National High Performance Computing Center (NHR@FAU) of the Friedrich-Alexander-Universitat Erlangen-Nurnberg (FAU) under the NHR project **<Your NHR@FAU project ID>**. NHR funding is provided by federal and Bavarian state authorities."

## BayernKI Project Resources

> "The authors gratefully acknowledge the scientific support and HPC resources provided by the Erlangen National High Performance Computing Center (NHR@FAU) of the Friedrich-Alexander-Universitat Erlangen-Nurnberg (FAU) under the BayernKI project **<Your NHR@FAU project ID>**. BayernKI funding is provided by Bavarian state authorities."

## Additional Requirements

Researchers should also submit copies of their publications to nhr-redaktion@lists.fau.de following resource utilization.
